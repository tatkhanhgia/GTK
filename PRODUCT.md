# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

GTKBlog serves Vietnamese and English-speaking developers and AI builders who want practical technical writing, project notes, tutorials, and digital products they can use in their own work.

Primary visitors arrive to read, evaluate the author's expertise, discover useful resources, or buy downloadable products. Authenticated areas, checkout, profile, downloads, and the Payload admin should switch to a product register because those screens serve task completion.

## Product Purpose

GTKBlog is a personal tech and AI publishing platform with a digital product store. It combines editorial blog content, author credibility, newsletter capture, secure checkout, and post-purchase downloads in one bilingual experience.

Success means visitors quickly understand the author's technical focus, trust the writing and products, move between Vietnamese and English without friction, and complete reading, subscribing, buying, or downloading flows with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Professional, simple, Anthropic-style.

The voice should feel calm, capable, precise, and useful. It should avoid hype while still showing strong technical taste. The public site can carry editorial confidence; product surfaces should become quieter and more operational.

## Anti-references

No project-specific anti-reference.

Avoid generic SaaS visual language, decorative glassmorphism, excessive gradients, fake complexity, noisy dashboards, and UI that feels more like a template than a personal technical publication. Do not let Anthropic-style become an excuse for bland cream cards everywhere.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with earned expertise. The interface should make writing, projects, and products feel credible before it asks users to act.
2. Keep bilingual reading comfortable. Vietnamese diacritics, English technical terms, long code-adjacent text, and localized commerce copy must all stay legible and calm.
3. Separate brand from workflow. Public pages can be editorial and expressive; checkout, profile, downloads, settings, and admin should prioritize clarity, state, and speed.
4. Use warmth with restraint. The coral, terracotta, cream, and warm dark palette should feel human and professional, not decorative.
5. Show real value. Prefer concrete article/product previews, author context, technical artifacts, and useful metadata over vague marketing copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public and authenticated surfaces. Maintain visible focus states, keyboard navigation, 44px minimum touch targets, semantic landmarks, descriptive alt text, and contrast-safe light and dark modes.

Respect reduced motion. Keep article bodies, code blocks, rich text, checkout steps, and admin workflows stable. Motion may support orientation and polish, but should never block reading or task completion.
